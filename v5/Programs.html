Questions

Quick answers.

Who should join which program?+

The Olympiad Program suits students working toward national and international Olympiads. The UG Mathematics Entrance program suits students preparing for CMI, ISI or foreign university entrance exams.

Do I need to prepare for Chapter Zero?+

No. It uses familiar school mathematics and shows how you think, so there is nothing to cram.

How do scholarships work?+

After both rounds, every student is placed in a Beginner or Advanced batch. Rankings happen inside each batch, and the top three earn scholarships of up to 100%, 50% and 25%. Scholarships apply to both programs.

How big are the batches?+

Very small, so mentors can read your proofs line by line and every student is heard.
