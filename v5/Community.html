Peer circles

Six students, one mentor, a lot of arguing. A circle is where you present your proofs and learn to critique others.

Think out loud, with people who push back.Circles are matched by level and topic after Chapter Zero, so everyone in the room is working at the same edge.

  2. STEP 02Get placedBeginner or Advanced, based on where your maths is today.

Guidelines & safety

A community for school students only works if it feels safe to be wrong. These are the ground rules.

  • Show your reasoningAn answer alone teaches nobody. Say what you tried first and why it worked.
  • Critique arguments, never peopleDisagree with the proof, not the person. Be kind about mistakes, including your own.
  • Keep spoilers awayToday’s problem stays hidden until midnight. Use the hint, not the answer, when helping.
  • Reviewed by mentorsLiminal mentors read posts and step in when a thread needs help or a reminder.
  • No private messagesStudents cannot message each other privately. Everything happens where mentors can see it.
  • Keep personal details outNo phone numbers, addresses or school names in posts. Use the report button if you see any.
