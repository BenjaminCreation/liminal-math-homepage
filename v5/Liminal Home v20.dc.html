NUMBER THEORY · LEVEL 3 · Q 4 OF 10How many positive divisors does 360 have?
360 = 2³ · 3² · 5Clean factorisation.
3 · 2 · 1 = 6Not quite. Each prime can appear 0 to k times.
(3+1)(2+1)(1+1) = 24Correct. 24 divisors.
Start by breaking 360 into primes.Try 12 = 2² · 3. List its divisors. How many choices for the power of 2?You fixed it yourself. That’s the skill we’re building.
