Why Round II?

The goal is not to separate students by ability. It’s to make sure every student is challenged without feeling overwhelmed.

Students arrive with very different experience. Round II brings harder, Olympiad-style problems and finds those ready for accelerated batches. Everyone else keeps developing at a pace that suits them.
